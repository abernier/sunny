Sunny

Privacy policy

Sunny collects no data.

Sunny is a menu-bar app that makes your Mac's XDR screen brighter. Everything it does happens on your Mac.

What Sunny does not do

  • No analytics, tracking or telemetry.
  • No network access: Sunny never connects to the internet.
  • No accounts, no sign-in.
  • No personal data is collected, stored, shared or sold.

What Sunny stores

Only the brightness level you last chose, saved locally on your Mac so Sunny can restore it. It never leaves your Mac.

Changes

Any change to this policy will be published on this page.
