Sunny

Your MacBook Pro's XDR screen goes brighter than macOS lets it. Sunny turns the sun up.

Download for Mac

macOS, Apple silicon, XDR display · GitHub

100%

drag the sun

Also in the terminal: luminosity 150, luminosity +, luminosity -, luminosity off. Each + is one x1.1 step, from 100 to 300%.

Not notarized yet: the first time, right-click Sunny and choose Open (or System Settings > Privacy & Security > Open Anyway).

A brighter screen runs warmer and drains the battery faster, and the brightest colours can clip. Needs a MacBook Pro 14" or 16" with Liquid Retina XDR display, M1 Pro or later.
